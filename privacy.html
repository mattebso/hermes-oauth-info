Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This policy covers the private Hermes Agent OAuth client operated by the repository owner for personal use.

Google data accessed

Depending on the permissions explicitly granted on Google’s consent screen, the local Hermes installation may access Gmail messages and drafts, Drive files, Calendar events, Docs, Sheets, and basic Contacts data. It uses this data only to perform actions requested by the authorized account owner, such as searching email, preparing drafts, reading or updating files, and managing calendar events.

Storage and processing

OAuth credentials and working data are stored on the owner’s local computer. Selected content may be sent to AI model providers configured by the owner when needed to perform a requested task. No Google user data is sold, used for advertising, or provided to data brokers.

Limited use

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and control

Data is retained only as needed for the owner’s local workflows and backups. Authorized users can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings. Local credentials and cached data can be deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions can be submitted through the repository issue tracker.
