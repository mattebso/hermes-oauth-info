Hermes Agent Personal OAuth

This is the information page for a private, personal installation of Hermes Agent, an open-source AI agent.

The OAuth client is used only by the owner and specifically authorized test accounts to connect Google Workspace services such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, and Contacts to the owner’s local Hermes installation.

Not a public service. There is no public signup, no customer offering, and no sale of Google user data.

How it works

Google authorization is initiated by the account owner. Access is limited to the scopes shown on Google’s consent screen and can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
